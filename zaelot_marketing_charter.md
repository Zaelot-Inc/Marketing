## 1. Purpose

Marketing helps Zaelot win and keep clients, and helps recruiting. LinkedIn activity should lead to identifiable client opportunities, not just engagement. **Agreed** (Fireflies 11-20).

## 2. Goals

Each goal gets a KPI. **Agreed** 11-23. Numbers: UNKNOWN until set.

| # | Goal | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grow revenue: more leads, including organic | Projects marketing influenced. First target: identify the first $1 USD attributed to or assisted by marketing (Jeff, 11-23) |
| 2 | Re-engage inactive contacts (HubSpot holds about 18,000, as stated 11-23) | Re-engagement activity that opens up stale relationships (Jeff, 11-23) |
| 3 | Win conferences with pre-event emails, post-event emails and matching LinkedIn messages (Jeff, 11-20) | Pre-event and post-event communication: number of events where communications are sent; total communications sent; total replies (Jeff, 11-23) |

| 6 | Serve recruiting: every Fryeah! talk becomes content (Jeff, 11-20) | HR-approved posts on LinkedIn (Jeff, 11-23) |

## 3. Channels

**Agreed** 11-20 and 11-23. Adding or dropping a channel is not Jeff's call; decider UNKNOWN.

| Channel | Its job |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn (company page and personal profiles) | Awareness and credibility. Prospects check it, and the website, before they engage. |
| Email to leads and clients (HubSpot) | Nurture. Includes the monthly Zaelot Beacon newsletter. |
| Event outreach | Win conferences. Follow-up matches the event: broad email for big shows, personal follow-up for small ones. Event owner decides. |
| Email to partners (HubSpot) | Keep partners informed. More customized than Beacon. |
| Account-based outreach (Crossbeam) | Warm intros through partners. |
| Website | Validation point for prospects. |

Open: Substack, Instagram, video (brief decisions 3 and 10).

## 4. Cadence

- **Marketing meetings:** Monday, Tuesday, Friday, about 30 minutes, same time each day. **Agreed** 11-23; Jeff to confirm the days. Run on the Sales War Room template in [marketing_war_room.md](marketing_war_room.md). **Agreed** (Jeff, 11-23). Standing agenda: Upcoming marketing, Ideas for posts, Ideas for emails, Interesting replies, Open action items, Next steps. **Agreed** (Jeff, 11-23).
- **LinkedIn:** 3 posts a day by Zaelot, 15 posts a week. Any of three count: (1) reposts, (2) posts by anyone on the sales team, (3) posts by the Zaelot company page. **Agreed** (Jeff, 11-23).
  - Earlier proposal, superseded: one post a day per contributor, about 200 words, to start; three a day across the team as the ideal; contributors claim topics from a shared list. Proposed 11-23 (Agustín, Germán). The shared topic list and the 200-word length still stand as working practice unless Jeff says otherwise.
- **End of every meeting:** an email summary of the meeting, generated with Fireflies. **Agreed** (Jeff, 11-23). Recipients and who sends it: UNKNOWN.
- **Beacon newsletter:** monthly. **Agreed** 11-23.
- **Metrics review:** first Friday of every month, on the prior month's data. **Agreed** (Jeff, 11-23). Replaces the earlier "first in December on November data" (11-23 call).

## 6. How content gets made and approved

- Writer drafts, the topic's final approver (section 5) OKs. Approval must be fast enough to keep the cadence. **Agreed** in principle 11-23; approvers by topic agreed (Jeff, 11-23).
- Internal material (Friday talks, client work) goes public only after client names and confidential detail are removed, and only with client approval where a client is named. **Agreed** 11-23.
- Outreach to someone's contacts is coordinated with that contact's owner first. **Agreed** 11-20.

## 7. Style

- Short and readable. The strongest line first. Few emojis or icons. **Agreed** 11-23.
- Useful perspective first, not service promotion. **Agreed** 11-23.
- One Zaelot voice. Voice guide: `Zaelot_Knowledge_Base/Zaelot-Brand-Voice.md` and the guide Agustín is getting. **Agreed** 11-23.
- No em dashes. Sourced facts only. Name a client only with approval. **Agreed** (repo house rules).
