# Zaelot Beacon, reference issue: "The Squads" (2021)

**Added:** 2026-11-23, as the reference for the monthly Beacon relaunch (initiative 21 in the marketing brief; owner Agustín).
**Source:** "Zaelot Beacon Newsletter - The Squads", sent 2021-08-19 from Mailchimp ("German from Zaelot"). Germán forwarded it to Agustín and Jeff on 2026-11-23. The original .eml stays out of this repo because the repo is public and the email holds personal data (intern names and ratings, a client contact's address, subscriber tracking links). It's in Jeff's Downloads folder.

## How the issue was built

| # | Block | What it said |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Subject and preheader | "Zaelot Beacon Newsletter - The Squads" / "Check out what a Zaelot Squad is." |
| 2 | Intro from the COO | Zaelot's view that the best way to approach a project is getting the right people working together, with the right support and management. |
| 3 | Explainer: What is a Squad? | Up to 8 professionals who share a tech stack, led by a Squad Captain (a veteran engineer in that stack). Members work on different clients but always have their squad behind them. The differentiator: hire one Zaelot engineer, get the support of the whole organization. |
| 4 | Explainer: Why Squads? | Flexibility, independence and support. |
| 5 | Personal sign-off | "My lines of communication are always open." Signed by Germán, COO. |
| 6 | Featured squad | Iron Martin (Python, Django, AWS, databases): how the squad chose its name, its stack, and the client work (a real estate company, staff augmentation, more than a year). Hiring note: new members coming. |
| 7 | Company updates | An award for a VueJS squad (SEBC Awards, innovation in UI and UX). A new internship partnership with PDX Code Guild (Portland bootcamp). |
| 8 | Bench: available people | Intern profiles: first name, background, skill ratings (stars per technology and English communication), "Available in N weeks". Interns offered to clients at no charge. |
| 9 | Footer | LinkedIn and website links, "You are in control.™" tagline, unsubscribe and preferences. |

## What worked

- **A client replied.** A client contact answered: "That's a great looking Squad!!!" (2021-08-19). On 2024-04-10 Jaki called it "really really good." On the 11-23 call it was named Zaelot's most successful past marketing motion; it generated profit (amount UNKNOWN) and stopped because it took too long to produce (Fireflies 11-23).
- **One idea per issue.** This issue explains one thing (squads) and features one squad.
- **The bench is the call to action.** Available people, with skills and a start date, under a "give me a call" line.
- **It comes from a person.** Written by the COO in the first person, with an open-door sign-off.
