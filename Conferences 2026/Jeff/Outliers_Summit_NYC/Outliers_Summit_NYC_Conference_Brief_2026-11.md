### Who is in the room

By title: 404 founders and CEOs, 27 C-suite, 52 VP/head/director, 28 partners and investors. Names stay private in the directory until you connect.

**Past events (organizer stats, 13 events):** 4,298 attendees from 3,083 companies; 48% of founders raised $1M or more; average raise among funded founders $5.9M.

**Read for Zaelot:** This is a founder crowd, not an enterprise buyer crowd. The fit is funded founders who need a product or AI build done fast and do not have the team: MVPs, agent and MCP integrations, web and app builds. Solo and small-team founders running on agents are the natural buyers for Zaelot's agent plumbing work. Investors (Weekend Fund, USVC, AngelList) are referral channels into portfolio companies.

### Intro playbook
1. **Before (by Tue Nov 3):** Set up the Pie profile. Use the same headline as LinkedIn plus one line on what Zaelot builds. Request Pie meetings with the A list and any enriched B-list founders.
2. **Pitch in one breath:** "We're the team founders call when they need an AI product or agent integration shipped and don't have the engineers. Senior LatAm teams, US time zones." (Confirm wording against current Zaelot positioning.)
3. **Qualifying question:** "What's the one thing on your roadmap you can't get built fast enough?"
4. **Capture:** After each conversation, log Status, Met on and Follow-up in the tracker the same day.
5. **Close the loop:** Ask for a 20-minute call the week of Nov 9 before walking away. Get a date, not "let's connect."
6. **After:** Update HubSpot (Andrew Yeung's title; new contacts) with Jeff's confirmation before any write. Draft follow-ups here in Layer 1 per house rules.
