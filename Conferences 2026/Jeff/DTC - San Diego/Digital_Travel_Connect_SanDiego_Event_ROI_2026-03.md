## The chairman effect

The commercial return above understates what this event bought. Three things happened here that no other 2026 event produced:

- **Zaelot chaired the conference.** Jeff opened Tuesday and ran through lunch. That is podium authority in the core vertical, not attendance.
- **A customer told our story from the stage, co-branded three ways.** Victor Davidson presented the Tripadvisor Segment-to-Hightouch migration on March 24 as Tripadvisor x Hightouch x Zaelot. Per the Tripadvisor brief, the headline results from that talk became **externally citable** as of that date, which converted internal case-study material into public proof.
- **Zaelot presented its own product.** The agentic coding tools got a speaking slot, with the CTO in the room.

**The deck is the second durable asset, and it was built jointly with Hightouch.** The talk was prepped in a four-way session with Victor Davidson (Tripadvisor), Kevin Swecker and Nate Wardwell (Hightouch), and Jeff. That collaboration produced value on three fronts, none of which appears in any pipeline figure:

- **Partnership depth.** Co-authoring a customer's on-stage story with a partner's analyst-relations and partner-marketing people is a different order of relationship than a co-sell referral. It is the kind of work that makes a partner treat you as a joint go-to-market motion.
- **A reusable sales asset.** The presentation has since been used in LinkedIn posts and internal sales documents. Built once for a single session, still in circulation five months later.
- **Publicly citable proof.** The Tripadvisor headline metrics became externally quotable as of 2026-03-24, which converted internal case-study material into evidence usable in any pitch.

There is also a direct line from this collaboration to a later event. **Nate Wardwell was in the DTC deck prep in March, and in July he presented Hightouch's marketing products at the Zaelot-hosted dinner in NYC.** The working relationship built around this deck is plausibly what made that possible.

Together the deck and the citable metrics are the event's real return. They have no expiry, they cost nothing further to use, and they are invisible in every funnel table in this report.

## Bottom line

Zaelot chaired the best-matched Travel and Hospitality audience of the year, had a customer present its story from the stage under three co-branded logos, and presented its own product. **All 84 delegates are in the CRM**, with the target list loaded before the floor opened.

Twelve delegates sit at Opportunity or better, at **$1,621 each** and **$232 per delegate in the room**. **No deal was created.** The net position is **-$19,448**.

Nineteen of those delegates were already in the database before 2026, and roughly 33 were bulk-imported afterward with no notes.

Two threads are genuinely live. **ALG Vacations**, where Dana Studebaker was met here and now sits on the recurring ALGV calls, against one of the most active account folders in the repo. And **Cruise Planners**, where Brian Shultz named his own Cartographer use case. Neither is quantified in a deal record, which is why this event reads worse on paper than it has actually performed.

The honest read: the podium and the roster were both excellent, two accounts are working, the deck and the citable Tripadvisor metrics are still earning five months on, and everything else in the room went untouched.

$15,000 of that spend went to a conference where Zaelot supplied the chairman, a product session, and a co-branded customer keynote. That fee should not be full price twice.

The durable assets are the jointly built Hightouch deck, still in use in posts and internal sales documents, and the publicly citable Tripadvisor results. Both originated here and neither expires. Judged only on recorded pipeline this event looks like a loss; judged on assets and partnership depth it is one of the better buys of the year.
