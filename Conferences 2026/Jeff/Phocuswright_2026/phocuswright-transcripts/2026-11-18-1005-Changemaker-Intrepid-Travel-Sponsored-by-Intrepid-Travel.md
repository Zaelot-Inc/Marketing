## Summary

The session opened with a framing of travel's biggest opportunity: most travelers can easily look up the best tapas restaurant in Barcelona, but what they really want is to shop at a local market and share a meal with a local family and new friends, an experience few businesses can actually deliver. Over more than 20 years, small-group adventure travel has shifted from a niche pursuit to something travelers increasingly demand, driven by a desire for adventure, human connection, memorable experiences, and confidence that their travel has a positive impact on the world.

Intrepid's growth was traced from carrying 47 people to Thailand in its first year of operation in 1989 to carrying more than 318,000 customers to over 118 countries last year, built on a vertically integrated model with its own offices in more than 35 countries, local tour leaders, and locally owned accommodation and restaurant partners that keep more tourism revenue in destination communities. Vietnam, where Intrepid opened its first country office in 2005 and now even operates its own hotel in Hoi An, was offered as an example of embedded local capability built over decades. The company is on track to surpass $1 billion in revenue next year, has tripled in size since before the pandemic, and posted its biggest booking month ever in October with 26% year-over-year growth in North America, a market seen as key to its 2030 targets.

Partnership was highlighted as central to scaling the Intrepid style of travel, with new collaborations bringing Intrepid trips to REI customers across North America and to Air Canada Vacations, pairing iconic consumer brands with Intrepid's local operational expertise. These partnerships were described as win-win: established brands gain a consistent, global, safety-vetted supply chain they couldn't build themselves, while Intrepid strengthens its ground network and contracting power. Travel advisors remain the majority distribution channel, valued for recommending immersive experiences and for wanting to align with trusted brands.

As a certified B Corp, Intrepid frames purpose and profit as mutually reinforcing rather than a cost: deeper investment in impact engages staff, engaged staff produce engaged customers (reflected in an NPS of 82), and engaged customers book more trips, generating returns that are reinvested into impact outcomes. This purpose focus was credited as a driver of the company's unusual growth rate for a 37-year-old business and as a magnet for attracting talented employees. An anecdote about a guide in Botswana's Chobe National Park thanking Intrepid for its role in Southern Africa's tourism ecosystem illustrated the company's emphasis on consistent presence and local supplier investment over sheer scale.

On technology, the discussion concluded that algorithms meant to connect people can instead drive polarization and loneliness, and that the industry's challenge is to combine its technology, distribution, and customer relationships with Intrepid's local teams and infrastructure to bring authentic experiences to more travelers. AI and automation were positioned as tools to speed up operations and resolve customer friction points and manual processes, while the human element—local guides and face-to-face connection—remains the irreplaceable core of the travel experience itself.

## Key Takeaways

### 💰 Local supply chains keep more tourism value in destinations

Working with locally owned accommodation, restaurants and businesses helps retain more of tourism’s economic benefits in the communities Intrepid visits.

Quotes:

> We work with locally owned accommodation, restaurants and businesses, and that allows us to keep more of the economic benefits of tourism with the places and the people that we visit.

### 🇺🇸 North America is central to Intrepid’s 2030 ambitions

Intrepid identifies North America as a key market for achieving its 2030 targets and reports that October was its strongest booking month in company history, with year-over-year growth of 26%.

Quotes:

> Now North America is key to us achieving our ambitious 2030 targets. And I'm really happy to report that October was the biggest booking month ever in our history. We grew 26% year over year.

### 🌱 Purpose is treated as a long-term commercial advantage

Intrepid says purpose and profit reinforce each other: investing in impact strengthens employee and customer engagement, supports its high NPS and repeat bookings, and ultimately contributes to financial returns.

Quotes:

> We don't see it as a short-term cost. We see it as a long-term strategic advantage. And I think one of the best things for us about having strong purpose and impact credentials is I attract way better people as a result.

### 🤝 Partnerships extend Intrepid’s global operating platform

Partnerships with established brands such as REI and Air Canada Vacations give them access to Intrepid’s globally consistent supply, local operations and health and safety protocols, while helping Intrepid expand its network and reach more customers.

Quotes:

> Established brands like REI as an example can come and contract with a business like Intrepid and we're able to provide a global solution to them that can add a great revenue stream to their business they wouldn't otherwise have the ability to deliver.

### 🏘️ Embedded local operations support scalable delivery

Intrepid’s model combines offices in more than 35 countries, local tour leaders, locally owned suppliers and company-operated trips to deliver destination experiences consistently at global scale—an infrastructure it says competitors cannot quickly replicate.

Quotes:

> It's a vertically integrated company with a global network of local expertise. At Intrepid, we have our own offices in more than 35 countries. We operate all of our own trips. Our tour leaders are locals.

### 🌍 Travelers increasingly seek local, human experiences

Many travelers want more than conventional sightseeing: they are seeking local markets, time in residents’ homes, new friendships, and experiences that create lasting human connections.

Quotes:

> People want adventure. They want human connection. They want experiences that stay with them long after they have returned from their traveling experiences.

### 🇻🇳 Vietnam shows the value of long-term local capability

Vietnam demonstrates how durable local teams and community relationships can strengthen a destination operation: Intrepid opened its first country office there in 2005, and many of the original team members remain.

Quotes:

> Many of the people that helped build that operation are still with us today. Bringing decades of local knowledge and community relationships.

### 🧑‍💼 Travel advisors remain a vital distribution channel

Despite the growth of technology and automation, Intrepid says the vast majority of its distribution still comes through travel advisors, who recommend immersive experiences and provide customers with trusted guidance.

Quotes:

> The key role of travel advisors for Intrepid is that still the vast majority of our distribution comes through travel advisors. They're the ones out there recommending our types of immersive experiential experiences. They want to work with a trusted brand.

### 🤖 AI can remove friction, but not replace human connection

Intrepid is applying technology and AI to manual processes and customer pain points so customers receive faster responses, while preserving in-person human connection as the core of on-the-ground delivery.

Quotes:

> We're implementing it to solve customer pain points and customer problems... And then the human component is incredibly important on the ground with delivery. People want that human connection.

### 🚀 Intrepid has paired purpose with substantial growth

After expanding from 47 travelers in its first year to more than 318,000 customers across over 118 countries last year, Intrepid says it has tripled in size since before the pandemic and is targeting more than $1 billion in revenue next year.

Quotes:

> Last year, Intrepid carried more than 318,000 customers to over 118 countries around the world... We're on track next year to do more than a billion dollars in revenue. We've tripled in size since before the pandemic.
