## Summary


## Key Takeaways

### 🤝 Combine AI speed with human trust

The panel sees the strongest travel model as a partnership: AI provides speed, information and discovery, while humans provide trust, judgment and care.

Quotes:

> The meeting in the middle of having the trust of the human with the speed of AI is kind of the right spot to be in.

### 🛫 Human support matters most when plans break

AI can provide useful travel information, but disruptions such as ground stops require a human who can coordinate rebooking, hotel extensions and next steps.

Quotes:

> Who’s going to extend your hotel? Who’s going to check when you can get on another flight? Who’s handling all of those things while we’re all here in a meeting?

### 🧑‍💻 Routine AI support frees humans for complexity

AI can resolve large volumes of straightforward advisor-support requests without reducing satisfaction, allowing human teams to concentrate on exceptional, ambiguous or high-stakes problems that require judgment.

Quotes:

> Our internal support ticket system runs on AI. We are answering over 40,000 inquiries every month with an over 90% satisfaction rating... that other set that really is complicated allows our support team to get involved.

### 🔄 Post-trip debriefs turn service into learning

An advisor’s value extends beyond booking and in-trip support: the quality of the post-trip debrief can distinguish a trusted advisor from a transactional agent by turning each journey into a deeper learning relationship.

Quotes:

> The single biggest differentiator that the customers themselves told us was the difference between a transactional travel agent and a trusted travel advisor was the quality of the post-trip debrief. And it becoming a learning experience.

### ⚠️ Verify AI against current expert data

AI can produce confidently incorrect travel information when it relies on generic or outdated web content, so expert-curated, current industry data and human judgment are essential for trustworthy answers.

Quotes:

> It told me A and the answer was B... It didn’t understand that they moved terminals six months ago... I think we have to raise the trust bar.

### 💳 Corporate trust is the next adoption barrier

For agentic AI to book and spend at scale, corporate buyers must be comfortable giving it fiduciary authority over company funds; the remaining barrier is institutional trust and control, not simply technology.

Quotes:

> That’s a CFO saying I’m allowing agentic to spend my money... I don’t know that CFOs are at the point where they’re ready to let AI at scale spend their money on their behalf.

### 📈 Embedded AI can multiply advisor productivity

When AI is integrated into an advisor platform, it can compress tasks such as creating client cards, drafting itineraries, producing quotes, analyzing business performance and answering routine support questions from hours to seconds. The panel linked active AI use with substantially higher advisor volume.

Quotes:

> Something that would have taken me an hour will take me 60 seconds... 96% of our top advisors are using Via on a daily basis... their volume is five times the ones that aren’t so active with AI.

### 🧠 AI cannot replace judgment and taste

AI can accelerate research and execution, but advisors still provide the judgment, taste, personal style and relationship-based understanding that shape a luxury client’s experience.

Quotes:

> I think there’s judgment. There’s taste... people deal with a particular advisor not because they’re technically great but because of their style.

### 💬 Human voice is becoming a premium

As AI increasingly handles service interactions, a genuinely human voice—and clarity about whether a caller is speaking with AI—will become part of the premium travel experience.

Quotes:

> Human voice the other line is a luxury good... I think travelers can and should tell the difference.

### 🔐 Advisors want automation with control

Advisors are open to AI handling booking, especially when they can monitor the process and retain clear intervention points rather than surrendering control to a traditional booking engine.

Quotes:

> The number one thing our advisor said they would actually like to use AI for is booking. But they have an assumption going in a booking that I can monitor a booking that I have access to.

### 💎 AI raises the value of expertise

AI makes information and basic knowledge easier to access, but high-quality expertise remains costly because it depends on curated data, judgment and experience rather than generic speed.

Quotes:

> Asking ChatGPT what’s the best hotel in London very quickly every single one will be in the heart of London... Good actual intelligence is quite expensive.
