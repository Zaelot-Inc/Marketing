## Summary

The pitch opened by framing a problem: loyalty benefits such as status, cards, and perks are invisible inside chat-based assistant conversations. Users are pushed out to websites via links or ads, forced to log in and navigate multiple steps, creating a poor experience focused on discovery rather than engagement. On the agent side, tools like Muse and Instinct scrape airline and hotel websites directly, meaning the brands have no way to recognize the user as a loyalty member or even verify they are a legitimate person, which makes suppliers uncomfortable with this kind of interaction.

The proposed solution is a platform that helps brands with large loyalty ecosystems and branded cards build loyalty experiences directly inside chat apps and for AI agents, avoiding the need to redirect users to a separate website. A demo showed booking a flight after logging in with loyalty credentials, with the ability to reserve, upgrade, and use miles within the chat conversation itself. By identifying the user and using a protocol-based connection rather than scraping, agents like Muse could apply a user's Platinum status, wallet card, and miles automatically when booking.

Technically, the platform addresses three needs for brands: a way for business teams (not engineers) to author conversational journeys that work across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other chat environments and devices, ready for publishing to plugin marketplaces; a reasoning engine that factors in partner ecosystems, loyalty status, and branded cards to serve the right offers and experiences back to the user in chat; and a management layer that lets brands see which platforms customers are coming through, what agents are doing, and what transactions—flights, rooms, partner programs—are being completed.

On traction and business model, the platform has been powering a major U.S. airline loyalty program for over a year with 100% availability and is SOC 2 certified, positioning it as enterprise-ready. The target market includes any airline, hotel, or cruise line with a large loyalty program and branded cards seeking direct engagement in chat platforms. Revenue comes from a platform fee plus activity-based charges, explicitly not a share of bookings.

In the Q&A, panelists pressed on how the model interacts with existing supplier and card-company loyalty programs and what value it adds beyond them; the response was that airlines and hotels want to engage customers directly in chat to retain margin, upsell related services like hotels or dining, and capture interchange fees by letting users book with a card stored in their wallet rather than entering card details into chat. Another question challenged how the platform could attract multiple loyalty programs without enough scale, and the answer clarified that the company works on behalf of its airline or hotel customers, connecting to their existing supply partners, booking systems, loyalty programs, and branded cards, and presenting that content into ChatGPT or other agents on the brand's behalf. A final question on reliability—whether a platinum member could trust the system to actually secure a better seat—was answered by explaining that logging in pulls the user's status, miles, and upgrade eligibility, which are then applied across all pricing and offers shown. The panel closed by thanking the presenter and applauding the pitch before moving to the next focus group.

## Key Takeaways

### ✍️ Business teams can author conversational journeys

Non-engineering teams can create the conversations customers experience across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other chat environments.

Quotes:

> We created the ability for business teams, not engineers, to author conversational journeys that will occur in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, across the different chat environments.

### 🤝 Direct chat engagement can expand supplier economics

Airlines and hotels may use direct in-chat engagement to transact with customers more directly, potentially reduce intermediary margin leakage and extend relevant offers to hotels, dining and other activities.

Quotes:

> If they book with you directly in chat, they don't have to go through a lot of the other layers, maybe not give up as much margin. Plus, after you book, they may say, well, now that you booked that flight, how about a hotel?

### 🧠 A reasoning engine selects relevant experiences

The platform’s reasoning engine considers partner relationships, loyalty status and branded-card information to return the appropriate experiences and offers in chat.

Quotes:

> It talks to a reasoning engine that looks at the partner ecosystem, the loyalty, the fact you have a branded card, and it serves back the right experience of partners, loyalty and offers back to the user in chat.

### ✅ Enterprise deployment demonstrates platform maturity

The platform has reportedly powered a major U.S. airline loyalty program for more than a year with 100% availability, and its commercial model combines a platform fee with activity-based fees rather than taking a share of bookings.

Quotes:

> We have been powering a major US airline loyalty program for over a year with 100% availability... Our business model is platform fee plus the level of activity we don't take a share of the bookings or things like that.

### 💳 Identity applies loyalty benefits and card context

When a system knows a customer’s loyalty tier and branded card, it can apply relevant benefits, miles and offers while giving the provider greater confidence in the interaction.

Quotes:

> It can know that I am a Platinum member and I have a card in my wallet, and it can go take advantage of all my benefits, use miles, get all the things that my loyalty program brings to me.

### 🌐 Cross-platform journeys support multiple devices and channels

Conversational journeys are designed to work across mobile and desktop and be prepared for publication through different plugin marketplaces and platform channels.

Quotes:

> It works across all different devices, mobile, desktop, things like that. And it will make it ready to publish to the various plugin marketplaces or platforms.

### 💬 Authenticated loyalty booking stays inside chat

Customers can log in within the conversational experience and complete benefits-rich transactions—such as booking flights, reserving upgrades and redeeming miles—without being redirected to a website.

Quotes:

> This is a demo of actually booking a flight after logging in with my credentials, and I can reserve, upgrade, use miles, do all sorts of things that bring my loyalty program into that conversation.

### 🛡️ Structured protocols replace anonymous website scraping

Emerging protocols let brands communicate with chat and reasoning agents in an identified, controlled way rather than relying on agents to scrape their websites.

Quotes:

> The protocols are there and they can be leveraged by companies.

### 🧩 Brands need authoring, integration and management tools

Supporting conversational commerce requires tools to author journeys, connect authentication with existing partner, booking, loyalty and branded-card systems, and monitor customer and agent activity.

Quotes:

> They need a way to build the conversation that's going to occur in chat. They need a platform to integrate with their authentication, with their partners, with their loyalty ecosystems. And they need a way to manage it to see what's going on.
