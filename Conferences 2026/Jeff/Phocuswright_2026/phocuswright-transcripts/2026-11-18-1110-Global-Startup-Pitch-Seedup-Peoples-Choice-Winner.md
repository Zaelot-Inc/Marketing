## Summary

The session opened with a pitch for Lobby, a startup built to help hotel sales and reservation teams handle complex, non-standard booking requests such as a wedding for 300 guests on half board with a split invoice and a contracted TUI rate. The pitch introduced Daniela, a fictionalized but real-inspired reservation manager who has worked at the same hotel for 18 years and holds all the institutional knowledge about contracts and exceptions, illustrating how operations break down when such staff are unavailable and how this problem recurs at every hotel Lobby onboards.

The core problem described is that despite the rise of OTAs, channel managers, and booking engines, a significant volume of bookings still arrive manually via email, PDFs, and tour operator portals, covering group requests, events, wholesale static contracts, and direct inquiries. This manual handling leads to low conversion, high operational costs, and error-prone processes, with skilled staff like Daniela spending most of their time on repetitive copy-and-paste work.

Lobby's solution consists of two modules: Automate, which converts incoming requests into actions like bookings, quotes, modifications, and cancellations, and Analyze, which checks existing reservations for errors and tracks team performance. The platform sits between communication channels (extranets, email, voice) and third-party systems of record such as PMS and CRM platforms, with new integrations taking days rather than weeks and onboarding typically completed within a day. Hotels retain control over what gets fully automated versus what requires human review before execution, and Lobby is designed to live directly inside existing inboxes (Outlook, Gmail, Zendesk) to minimize friction and switching costs, driving broader adoption. The stated benefits are faster responses, higher conversion, and improved efficiency, allowing portals to scale volume without added headcount and attracting more business from partners due to faster response times.

The team sees this operational problem as extending beyond hospitality into other travel verticals, and the underlying engine is being adapted to serve them even while the current focus remains on hotels. Commercially, Lobby is already working with multi-property setups, management companies, operators, and franchise networks across the Indian Ocean, Gulf region, Switzerland, and the Caribbean, and has white-labeled its technology for an attractions and experience platform. The company raised $2.2 million in pre-seed funding this year, with GetYourGuide co-founder Pascal Mathis joining the board.

The founding team's background includes four years building core banking software together before Lobby, with one founder as an engineer and another as a product owner, plus a third team member with Silicon Valley AI startup experience; advisors include the former CTO of Accor. The closing message grounded the company's mission in a personal story: Daniela was inspired by the real reservation manager of the presenter's family hotel, framing Lobby's purpose as making complex booking effortless, followed by an invitation for hotels, partners, or interested attendees to connect afterward.
