## 9. Day 1 takeaways (said on stage, not verified)
- The sessions kept coming back to data ownership and agent readiness: clean data, MCP and agent protocols, identity. That is Zaelot's pitch.

- United is blocking third-party AI agents; Hilton says it will work with every agent platform.
