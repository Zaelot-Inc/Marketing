**Inntopia predates both shows by more than a year.** The Inntopia brief states the relationship was "built over a year of informal contact at ski industry conferences (Aspen, NSAA)," with the first formal Zaelot meeting on 2026-03-17. No Inntopia record traces to February 2026.

**On the strategy conclusion, the recap is directionally right for the wrong reason.** It argues the NSAA shows are "prime targets again" because Alterra closed. The better argument is in this event's own numbers: nine contacts, two reaching SQL or opportunity, and a $147,840 deal, from a two-day regional show. That is a real return profile. ## What this says about event strategy

This event worked, and the recap undersells it. Nine contacts from a two-day regional show, captured cleanly within 48 hours, producing one SQL, one Opportunity with 37 notes, and a $147,840 deal. The capture discipline here is the model: a single import batch two days after the floor closed.

What did not work is everything after capture. The deal lost in four weeks with no debrief, five contacts were never touched, neither SQL has a brief, and the strongest contact on the circuit does not appear in the year-end recap. The event generated real material and the follow-through did not match it.

The 2027 question is not whether to return. On this evidence it is worth returning. The question is whether the same nine contacts would get worked this time.
