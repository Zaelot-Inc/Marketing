## The "warm-up lists" claim, tested

The recap describes this as "the second of the two NYC Braze events that produced real leads, now sitting in lists and being warmed up on an ongoing basis."

Neither Braze event has a single traceable HubSpot contact. Two events are credited with feeding the same lead pool and that pool has no members in the CRM.

The 65-contact list clearly exists, because the recap reports precise figures from it: 65 contacts, 17 companies, 6 confirmed, invited-versus-confirmed tracked. Somebody built and worked a real spreadsheet. The issue is not that the work was not done. It is that the work lives somewhere only one person can reach, which means:

- nobody else can follow up on it
- it does not appear in any pipeline review or forecast
- it cannot be cross-referenced against existing accounts, as the Royal Caribbean case demonstrates
- it disappears entirely if that person changes roles

September's FORGE event is the next step in this arc and is meant to put this contact list to work with the wider Braze customer base. Doing that from outside the CRM repeats the same problem a third time.

## Data integrity issues found while building this

1. **The 65-contact outreach list is not in HubSpot.** No contact from any of the 17 invite companies was created around the workshop date.
2. **The 6 confirmed attendees are unidentified.** The most valuable output of the event and there is no accessible record of who they were.
3. **No Braze partner contacts exist in HubSpot at all**, despite six-plus certified staff and two events co-hosted with Braze this year.
4. **The Royal Caribbean cross-sell cannot be actioned** because the confirmed Braze contact is unnamed.
5. **The certification sprint cost is unrecorded**, so the real investment behind this room is unquantified.
6. **No event source tagging exists on any record**, portal wide, which is the root cause across this whole report series.

## What this says about event strategy

This event had the best pre-work of the year and the worst measurability. Both statements are true and they are not in tension: the outreach list, the company targeting, and the confirmed-versus-invited tracking are exactly the discipline missing from every other event in the series. Sixty-five named contacts across seventeen well-chosen companies is real prospecting work.

It is all stranded outside the CRM. The result is an event that almost certainly produced value, sitting at zero on every measure this report can access, with a live cross-sell into a $1.25M account that cannot be actioned because the relevant name is not written down anywhere shared.

The fix is a single afternoon of data entry and it is worth doing before FORGE in September. The Braze motion is the most methodically run partner relationship Zaelot has: certifications, three events, Braze's own office. It deserves to be measurable.
