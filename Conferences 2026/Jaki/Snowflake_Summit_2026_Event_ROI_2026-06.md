## What this says about event strategy

This event returned a new logo and $126,700, which makes it the most financially productive event on the 2026 calendar on current evidence. That conclusion holds.

But the mechanism deserves precision, because the wrong lesson is easy to draw. The return did not come from attending, from the giveaway, from the ad spend, or from the eight contacts collected on the floor. It came from a customer publicly crediting us from a stage while a prospect sat in the audience. That is earned credibility converting in real time, and it is a fundamentally different play from trade-show lead capture.

The strategic read: the highest-return activity available to us at a conference is getting a happy customer on stage, not working the floor. That is repeatable, it is cheaper than a booth, and it is the thing to build the 2027 plan around. The floor capture at this event produced eight untouched Lead records and zero dollars.
