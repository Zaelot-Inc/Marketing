## The "real leads" claim, tested

The recap makes a specific claim about this event: "Real leads came out of this one, now captured in lists and being warmed up on an ongoing basis, not a one-time follow-up."

The leads are not in HubSpot. That is the finding, and it needs to be read carefully, because there are two very different explanations and they call for opposite responses.

**Explanation one: the leads exist in a tool outside the CRM.** The recap says "lists," and Braze events plausibly produce a shared attendee list or a marketing-automation segment rather than individual CRM records. If so, the leads are real and the problem is purely visibility: nothing in HubSpot means nothing in any forecast, any pipeline review, or any report like this one.

**Explanation two: the leads are informal.** Conversations that felt productive, business cards, LinkedIn connections, no structured capture. In that case "being warmed up on an ongoing basis" is describing an intention rather than a process.

**We cannot tell which from the CRM alone, and that is the problem.** Both the May event and the July Braze workshop are credited in the recap with producing "the leads now sitting in our warm-up lists." Neither has a single traceable HubSpot contact. Two events, one claim, zero records.

Until someone names the list and its location, this event has no measurable output, and its stated primary outcome cannot be verified or valued.

## Data integrity issues found while building this

1. **No event source tagging exists on any record.** There is no campaign, source, or custom property marking a contact as event sourced, anywhere in the portal. Every funnel figure in this report series is inferred from creation-date windows, which is why claims like this one cannot be checked.
2. **The "warm-up lists" are unlocated.** Two events are credited with feeding them. Nobody outside Jaki can currently find them, open them, or count them.
3. **No Braze partner contacts exist in HubSpot at all.** Given six or more certified staff and two events hosted with Braze this year, the absence of any Braze-side contact record is itself a gap. Partner relationships that live only in one person's inbox do not survive that person changing roles.

## What this says about event strategy

There is a real partner relationship here. Braze hosted us in their own office, six or more of our people are certified, and a third event is on the calendar for September. None of that is in doubt.

What is in doubt is whether this specific event produced anything countable. The recap's central claim for it, real leads in warm-up lists, has no corresponding record in the CRM, and the same claim is made for the July workshop. Two events cannot both be the source of a lead pool that has no members.

The fix is small and worth doing before FORGE in September. Find the lists, import them, tag them. A partner motion with three events and zero contact records is producing relationship value that cannot be measured, forecast, or defended in a budget conversation.
