## Bottom line

$298 bought a partner-funded product launch to an audience Zaelot assembled: one customer, two live opportunities, three warm leads, all followed up within six days. Hightouch paid 85% and brought the content.

No pipeline resulted. The one deal in the room lost three weeks later, no CDP opportunity was opened at 1stDibs, and the two best-fit invitees who declined were never sent a recap.

The format works and is underused at once a year. The failure is in the week after, not the evening itself.
