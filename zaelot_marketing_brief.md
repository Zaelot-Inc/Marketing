## 3. What marketing is for

*From the 11-20 "Marketing for Zaelot" call (Jeff, Jaki, Agustín, Germán). Fireflies [01M7VPERQYMJ9G3PFE1GD279KN](https://app.fireflies.ai/view/01M7VPERQYMJ9G3PFE1GD279KN).*

### Goals
1. **Grow revenue.** More leads, including organic ones, and marketing that makes sales activity more focused.
2. **Re-engage inactive contacts.** Dormant prospects, past clients, conference contacts and partners.
3. **Win conferences** with pre-event emails, post-event emails and matching LinkedIn messages. (Jeff, 11-20.)
4. **Nurture who we already know.** Keep regular contact with existing prospects and customers, moving them from cold to warm without needing a sales call each time.
5. **Be thought leaders in our verticals.**
6. **Serve recruiting too.** 44% of LinkedIn followers are engineers and many are entry level, so content has to work for job seekers as well as buyers.

### What each channel is for

| Channel | Its job | What we said on the call |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Awareness and credibility | Agustín: prospects check our page, so it has to look active. LinkedIn is not expected to be the main source of new clients. Core content: company updates, events, milestones, partner news. Idea from Jeff's notes: try video. |
| **Email to leads and clients (HubSpot)** | Nurture | Seen as the most effective channel for people who already know us. Should connect with what we post on LinkedIn. The Thanksgiving email was the example of a good relationship note: it reminds non-customers we're here without asking for anything. |
| **Event outreach** | Win conferences | Conference contacts are valuable leads. Each event gets a pre-event email, a post-event email and matching LinkedIn messages. |
| **Email to partners (HubSpot)** | Keep Zaelot top of mind with partners | Regular updates on events, thought leadership and company news. Jeff pointed to Cloudinary's partner emails as the model. Partners: Amplitude, Braze, Cloudinary, Contentful, FullStory, Hightouch, Sanity, Vercel, Insider, Salesforce. |
| **Account-based outreach** | Warm intros through partners | Crossbeam: 179 partner prospects and 93 Zaelot prospects who are already a partner's customers (as stated on the call). |

### Where we are
- About 3 LinkedIn posts a month. Results are positive; milestone, partner, Hightouch and Databricks posts did best.
- The audience is mostly engineering and Latin American, light on buyers.

### What's holding us back
- **Production, scheduling and consistency.** Jeff and Jaki are both short on time; someone needs to own creating and scheduling content.
- **Coordination.** LinkedIn and email aren't planned together, and lists need clearer segments and owners.
- **Review.** Jeff wants to see who gets marketing email. Outreach to someone's contacts gets coordinated with that owner and reviewed before it sends.

### What we agreed
- **Strategy before volume.** Cadence ideas ranged from 1 post and 1 email a week to 2 posts a week, but Jeff asked to agree purpose, audiences, messages and success measures first. The test: LinkedIn activity should lead to identifiable client opportunities, not just engagement.
- **Next:** the call paused for the Friday session and resumes after it.

**What the baseline says:**
1. Reach is paid. The boosted Phocuswright post ("Fort Lauderdale, here we come", 11-11) got 183,619 impressions but 2 reactions and 52 clicks.
2. Partner and milestone news performs best: Hightouch Certified Composable Partner (56 reactions, 10 reposts), Esri partner (27), Claude skill for Hightouch (23, 8 reposts).
3. Being at the event beats announcing it. Best organic post: Digital Travel Connect wrap, 2,221 impressions, 49 reactions.
4. Image posts 3.04% engagement rate vs link posts 0.76%.
5. The followers are mostly engineers (Zaelot's talent market), not buyers. BD and Sales are a bigger share of page visitors than of followers.
6. Missing from the feed: client case studies, engineering how-tos, the AI and MCP sales-stack story.

**Cadence discussed 11-20:** at least 1 LinkedIn post a week, then 2 a week as a baseline, against the report's 3 a day. Not decided; Jeff wants the strategy agreed first. (Fireflies 11-20.)
