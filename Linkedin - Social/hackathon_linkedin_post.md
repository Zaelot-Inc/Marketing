On Tuesday, Jaki Levy and I co-hosted an Ad Studio Hackathon with Hightouch at their beautiful new NYC office. The brief from the invite was simple: no slides, no sales pitch. You build, we help.

The best part was who was in the room. Tejas Manohar, Hightouch's co-founder and co-CEO, opened the session himself, the day after launching their new Customer Engagement Platform and in between press interviews about it. Alec Haase (GM, AI Products), Nate Argosh (Product) and Trevor Sutley (Ad Studio) were right there helping our guests build.

Tejas set it up in one line: Hightouch is the agentic marketing company. Marketing should move at the speed of ideas, and the ideas should keep coming.

The problem is familiar to anyone who runs paid media. Creative demand outpaces capacity:
- 10x more creative volume is needed than three years ago
- Creative diversity drives a 32% increase in Meta conversions
- Only 6 to 7% of ads launched actually win

Most teams answer with templates, and templates all look the same. Hightouch's squint test: if you squint and the ads look alike, they are not diverse enough, and the ad platforms know it.

What stuck with me was their take on context. AI agents are only as good as what they know about your business, so Ad Studio pulls together four layers: business context (regulations, disclosures), marketing context (channels, trends, customers), analytical context (performance) and brand context (fonts, colors and "a certain feeling"). Then a team of agents runs the paid media loop, measure, ideate, create, launch, with people reviewing and approving along the way.

Then everyone built. In two hours, marketers built on-brand ads from scratch, with challenges like building 50 ads, animating an ad and riffing on a competitor's ad. Watching people go from "let me try something" to a stack of usable creative was the whole point.

Thank you to Tejas, Alec, Nate, Trevor, Noah Rahimzadeh, Robert Yula, Noel and the whole Hightouch team for hosting, and to every brand that came out to build with us. Proud to be your partner.
