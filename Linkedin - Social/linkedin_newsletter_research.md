# LinkedIn newsletter: research

**Created:** 2026-11-23, after Jeff proposed a weekly LinkedIn newsletter that packages our posts and asks people to subscribe (brief decision 11). **Status:** research only. Nothing is decided.

## The question

When Jeff follows a brand (example: ZentrumHub), LinkedIn offers him its newsletter ("ZentrumHub invited you to subscribe to Hotel API Insider", weekly). Could Zaelot do the same, and who would get our invite?

## How the invite works

- **Who gets it:** when a Page publishes the first edition of a newsletter, LinkedIn invites the **Page's followers** by notification to subscribe. Publishing that edition also creates a newsletter page, listing the editions. ([LinkedIn Help: Create a LinkedIn Page newsletter](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a596833))
- **After that:** subscribers get an in-app notification and an email for every new edition. ([LinkedIn Help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a596833))
- **New followers:** LinkedIn also invites people who follow the Page later. (Vendor guides, [linkedinpreview.com](https://linkedinpreview.com/blog/linkedin-newsletter-growth-2026); not confirmed in LinkedIn Help.)
- **What this means for us:** the invite goes to people who follow **us**. Zaelot following 278 companies (11-23) doesn't invite those companies' people to our newsletter. It does put their newsletters in front of us, so it's useful for research.

## Can Zaelot's page publish one?

- LinkedIn's criteria for Pages: more than 150 followers and/or connections, recent original posts, and a page and admins in good standing. Meeting them makes a page eligible for review, not guaranteed access. ([LinkedIn Help: Newsletter access criteria](https://www.linkedin.com/help/learning/answer/134850))
- Zaelot's page has 5,574 followers (brief section 3, 11-20 baseline) and posts about 3 times a month. Whether the newsletter tool is already turned on for our page: UNKNOWN. Agustín or another page admin can check in the page's admin view.

## Who our invite would reach

- 5,574 followers, but 44% are engineers, many entry level. Buyers are a smaller share (brief section 3). The first invite would reach mostly our talent market, not buyers.
- To reach buyers, they need to follow the page first. Sources: the company follows and HubSpot contacts (initiative 22), event follow-ups, and sales team posts that point to the newsletter.

## Cadence and measurement

- **Cadence:** Jeff: most LinkedIn newsletters are weekly. One vendor guide suggests every two weeks for B2B. Guides agree a predictable schedule matters most. ([trymypost.com](https://www.trymypost.com/blog/linkedin-newsletter-strategy-b2b-growth-2026))
- **Metrics:** LinkedIn added email sends and open rates to newsletter analytics in February 2025. No click-through or churn data per that source. ([pinkdogdigital.com](https://pinkdogdigital.com/linkedins-new-newsletter-metrics-email-sends-open-rates))
- **Open rates:** vendor claims run from 25% to 60%, and LinkedIn measures opens differently from email tools. Not reliable. Don't put a target on it until we have our own data.

## Fit with what we have

- **Beacon** is a monthly HubSpot email to contacts we choose (Agustín, 11-23). A LinkedIn newsletter would go to anyone who subscribes. They can share content: the weekly edition packages the week's posts; Beacon picks the best of the month.
- **Weekly needs about one post a day** to have enough to package. That matches the 11-23 cadence (charter section 4).
