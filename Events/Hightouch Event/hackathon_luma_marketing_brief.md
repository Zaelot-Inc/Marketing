## 4. Messaging

**Hook (Noah's LinkedIn post):** how long does it take your team to get one ad from idea to every placement it needs to run in? For most marketers, longer than it should: briefs, revisions, resizes, more revisions.

**Core promise (same post):** in two hours, generate on-brand ads from scratch, refine with prompts or the built-in editor, and resize across placements in minutes. "No slides, no sales pitch. You build, we help."

**Pillars:**
1. **Hands-on, not a demo.** About 30 minutes of walkthrough from Nate Argosh, then attendees build and iterate with hosts helping.
2. **Your brand, not a template.** Attendees who send brand guidelines and assets get a branded sandbox. Jaki committed to brand-context support for every attending brand where possible; a generic sandbox is the fallback.
3. **Works without the Hightouch CDP**, including for Segment customers (agreed 10-07). Lead with technical credibility.
4. **Everyone leaves with something.** Noah prefers an inclusive format over a contest that leaves people feeling they lost. Attendees present their work on the room monitor at the end.
5. **Incentive:** a pair of **Knicks tickets** (provided by Jeff's team). Agreed to mention it in outreach.

**Social proof:** happy hour "alongside marketers from Hightouch, Zaelot, and a handful of top brands."

**Referral ask:** "Know someone who'd get a lot out of this? Tag them below." Reuse in reshares and DMs.

**Avoid:** sales-heavy language, "competition" framing, overpromising custom sandboxes to late registrants.

### 5f. Hightouch blog: agentic marketing platform comparison (pasted by Jeff, 2026-10-18; publish date UNKNOWN)

URL: https://hightouch.com/blog/best-agentic-marketing-platforms

Hightouch's own comparison of seven vendors (Hightouch, Salesforce, Adobe, HubSpot, Braze, Iterable, GrowthLoop) across five platform types, judged on six criteria: what the agents work from, where they act, launch vs recommend, where data sits, approvals and audit, and buying one piece.

**What it gives the Luma page:**
- **Host boilerplate:** "the Agentic Marketing Platform, powered by the industry-leading Composable CDP." Ad Studio agents build creative from approved assets and brand rules, ready to launch on existing ad platforms.
- **Proof point:** Otrium went from about four weeks brief-to-live to one week with Ad Studio, conversions up 15%.
- **Why brand assets matter:** Hightouch's "brand context layer" (guidelines, approved assets, past work) is what the agents look up. Sending assets before the event is how attendees get ads in their own brand. Content Assembly reuses approved assets instead of generating new imagery.
- **Governance:** a person signs off on every AI-generated asset before it runs. Useful for brand and creative leads who worry about off-brand AI output.
- **Buy one piece:** either Studio, or the CDP, can be bought on its own. This backs the 10-07 line that Ad Studio works without the Hightouch CDP.

**For conversations at the event (not for the page):**
- **Ad Studio insights:** agents analyze past ad performance, competitor ads, and industry trends, and flag creative fatigue or a competitor ad gaining traction.
- **EWTN:** EWTN chose GrowthLoop over Hightouch CDP. The post's own "what to check" for GrowthLoop: it sends audiences to other tools, so ask how much of the content and launch work its agents handle. Ad Studio covers the creative and launch side GrowthLoop does not, which fits the workshop-to-pilot path with more pricing room on Ad Studio.
- **Six demo questions** from the post (what can the agent see, can it launch, where does data sit, which channels, GA vs beta, approval points) make a good discussion prompt during show and tell.
- **Honest caveat from Hightouch itself:** getting the most from Hightouch takes setup up front (connecting data, loading brand guidelines and assets). That is a Zaelot services opening after the event.

### 5g. Ad Studio product page (pasted by Jeff, 2026-10-18; URL UNKNOWN, likely hightouch.com/platform/ad-studio)

**Page headline:** "Never run out of on-brand ads again." Three steps:
1. **Spot the signals and act immediately:** turn signals from performance data, the market, and competitors into hundreds of concepts in minutes. Refresh fatiguing creative, react to cultural trends, respond to competitor ads.
2. **Edit the way you want, with AI or visually:** review, refine, and resize with prompts or a built-in editor. (The live Luma page in 5a reuses this wording: "so every ad has your finishing touch.")
3. **Launch to any ad platform:** 100+ destinations listed, incl. Meta, Google Ads, TikTok, LinkedIn Ads, Snapchat, Pinterest, Reddit, The Trade Desk, Amazon Ads DSP, ChatGPT Ads (OpenAI).

A Times Square cover variant is a possible next design.

**Brand foundation:** creative built in layers; connected to existing assets in Adobe, Figma, Google Drive, and Dropbox; product catalog integration for product accuracy; brand guidelines panel (logo, fonts, colors, components); export to Figma.

**Governance (answers the brand-safety worry, now a short FAQ in 5c):** ISO 27001 certified; every AI-generated asset needs sign-off; configurable brand and content rules applied automatically; GDPR and data-residency compliance incl. AI disclosures; customer brand assets, data, and campaigns never train the underlying models.

**For the event:** step 1 (signals, fatigue, competitors) is the strongest demo for performance marketers; ask Nate whether the sandboxes include trend and competitor analysis, or only generation and editing.
