**Cadence**

- **Monday, plan the week:** posts planned, emails to send, events coming up, open action items.
- **Wednesday, check-in:** what posted, what's stuck in review, LinkedIn replies, urgent event messages, open action items.
- **Friday, weekly wrap:** what shipped, what slipped, next week's topics, open action items. First Friday of the month adds the metrics review.

**Rules (from the Sales War Room README)**

1. Short entries: one or two sentences of status per item. Detail goes in the brief.
2. No internal 1:1 content.
3. Facts, owners, dates. No commentary.
4. No em dashes.
5. Open action items never age out; only closure removes them. Close with status `Done` and the outcome.
6. Every agenda carries the full open action items table (section 6).

**Standing agenda, every session, in this order (Jeff, 11-23):**

- **Headline:** one line.
1. **Upcoming marketing:** events, launches and campaigns on the [marketing calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/embed?src=c_d5f9ae49473766f07b343cfc79a81097c72353da22d2e4e41d2c7b566dd2b2a0@group.calendar.google.com&ctz=America/New_York).
2. **Ideas for posts.**
3. **Ideas for emails.**
4. **Posts this week:** what Zaelot and the team posted on LinkedIn since the last meeting, with links (Jeff, 11-24).
5. **Interesting replies:** notable responses to posts, emails and outreach.
6. **Open action items:** table with item, owner, date added, status. Carried forward until done.
7. **Next steps:** dated, nearest first.

**Close every meeting:** send an email summary of the meeting, generated with Fireflies (Jeff, 11-23). Then update this doc from the same recording: archive the session, prep the next one, carry open items.

Anything that doesn't fit these sections goes to the [brief](zaelot_marketing_brief.md) (reference) or the [history file](marketing_war_room_history.md), verbatim. Structural decisions go to the [Zaelot Marketing Charter](zaelot_marketing_charter.md).
